# HTA vs HTD comparison (Milestone 3)

Steps are counted as **leaf operations**, which are boxes with no children. A parent box is not counted, because its work is done by its children. HTD counts come from `Teacher_HTDs.drawio`.

## Teacher

### HTA 1 (grade a paper exam) → HTD T1 (Grade a class set of papers)

| Part | HTA 1 steps (today) | HTD T1 steps (Sprout) |
|---|---|---|
| Get the papers | 2 (1.1-1.2) | 3 (1.1-1.3: open the papers) |
| Prepare to mark | 2 (2.1-2.2) | 0 |
| Check the writing / text | 4 (3.1, 3.2.1-3.2.3) | 4 (2.1-2.4: check the text) |
| Mark each task | 5 (3.3, 3.4.1-3.4.3, 3.5) | 3 (3.1-3.3) |
| Total and grade | 4 (4.1-4.4) | 2 (3.4 check the total and grade, 4 press "Confirm grade") |
| Review the class | 1 (5) | 0 |
| **Total** | **18** | **12** |

- **Collecting and carrying papers home (1.1-1.2) disappears.** Students send photos of their exam (HTD S1), so the teacher just opens the class set under "Papers".
- **Preparing the answer key and points (2.1-2.2) disappears.** The key and points are set once, when the assignment is created (HTD T3), and stay one click away under "Answer key".
- **Unclear writing no longer needs guessing or asking the child the next day (3.2.1-3.2.3, 3.3).** Only the words the AI is unsure about are highlighted, with the scan beside them, and reversed digits and crossed-out work are flagged. The teacher picks the right reading.
- **Giving points step by step and ticking in green (3.4.1-3.5) becomes "read the AI mark and the reason".** The teacher changes a mark only if they disagree.
- **Adding up, re-checking and converting to a grade (4.1-4.3) is automatic.** The total and the German 1-6 grade are worked out by the system, and the teacher only checks them (3.4). The comment (4.4) moves to T4 as the two stars and a wish. The class overview (5) is the class page's status summary, so it is no longer a separate task.

### HTA 2 (record and return the results) → HTD T4 (Return the results)

| Part | HTA 2 steps (today) | HTD T4 steps (Sprout) |
|---|---|---|
| Open the step | 0 | 1 (1 open the "Release" step) |
| Record the marks | 5 (1.1-1.3, 1.4.1-1.4.2) | 0 |
| Feedback on the paper | 4 (2.1-2.4) | 5 (2.1-2.2, 3.1-3.3) |
| Hand back the papers | 2 (3.1-3.2) | 3 (4.1-4.3), then 1 (5 press "Release") |
| Correction lesson | 6 (4.1-4.2, 4.3.1-4.3.3, 4.4) | 0 |
| Involve the parents | 3 (5.1-5.3) | 0 (4.2 keeps "Send Tonight's Activity" ticked) |
| **Total** | **20** | **10** |

- **Writing marks into the grade book, Excel and the tracker (1.1, 1.2, 1.4.1-1.4.2) disappears.** Marks are recorded automatically when the grade is confirmed. Entering term grades in the school portal (1.3) is not part of T4.
- **Circling the wrong step and writing the right one (2.1-2.2) becomes approving the AI's Mistake Replay circle.** The teacher can press "Move" or "Don't show" if it is wrong. The two stars and a wish (2.3) are drafted for the teacher to edit, and the sticker (2.4) is one button.
- **Handing back papers and keeping grades private (3.1-3.2) becomes one "Release".** Each child gets only their own result, and "Show the grade" controls whether the grade is shown at all.
- **The one-to-one correction lesson (4.1-4.4) is replaced by Mistake Replay.** The child replays their own mistakes on their device (HTD S2), so the teacher does not have to sit with each child.
- **Sending papers home and collecting signatures (5.1-5.2) disappears.** Parents see the result in the parent app (HTD P2) and get Tonight's Activity (HTD P1). Parents' questions (5.3) go through messages (HTD P3).

### HTA 3 (identify and support a struggling child) → HTD T2 (Confirm or dismiss a red flag)

| Part | HTA 3 steps (today) | HTD T2 steps (Sprout) |
|---|---|---|
| Notice a warning sign | 4 (1.1-1.4) | 1 (1 open the "Red flags" step) |
| Look for a pattern | 6 (2.1, 2.2.1-2.2.3, 2.3, 2.4) | 2 (2.1-2.2: review the flag) |
| Rule out a one-off | 2 (3.1-3.2) | 3 (3.1-3.3: decide) |
| Try support first | 3 (4.1-4.3) | 0 |
| Escalate | 3 (5.1, 5.2.1-5.2.2) | 0 |
| Save | 0 | 1 (4 press "Save flag decision") |
| **Total** | **18** | **7** |

- **Spotting the warning sign yourself (1.1-1.4) disappears.** The AI raises a red flag with the skill and a confidence level while the papers are graded.
- **Searching notebooks, past tests and the tracker for a pattern (2.1-2.4) disappears.** The flag shows the evidence from the child's own answers across tests, so the teacher only reads it (2.1-2.2).
- **Ruling out a one-off (3.1-3.2) becomes one decision.** The teacher chooses "Confirm" or "Dismiss" with a reason, or "Keep watching" until the next test.
- **First support (4.1-4.3) is started by the decision.** Confirming a weakness can send Tonight's Activity to the parents. Release and sending the activity are now in T4.
- **Escalation (5) is not modelled in T2.** The teacher can leave a note (for example, "talk to the learning support teacher first") for later.
